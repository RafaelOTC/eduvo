## Overview

**Eduvo** is an all-in-one SaaS platform for tutoring teachers who want to run their activity from a single place instead of juggling messaging apps, spreadsheets, calendars, video platforms and separate admin tools.

The product is designed around the real day-to-day workflow of a tutor: finding students, accepting bookings, organizing lessons, communicating, tracking activity and teaching online.

> **Current stage:** Open Beta — actively tested and developed.

## Why Eduvo exists

Private tutoring is usually managed through a patchwork of disconnected tools.

Eduvo brings those workflows together into one coherent workspace:

| Area | What Eduvo brings together |
| --- | --- |
| Scheduling | Calendar, lessons, availability and booking |
| Students | Active/inactive student management and profiles |
| Discovery | Public teacher profiles and teacher discovery |
| Communication | Internal messaging and notifications |
| Teaching | Online lessons, homework, resources and workflows |
| Trust | Reviews and public-facing teacher information |
| Roles | Separate flows for teachers, students and parents |
| AI | **LUNA**, Eduvo's contextual assistant |

## Core product capabilities

### Scheduling & booking
- advanced lesson calendar
- two-way booking flow
- booking request → confirmation → lesson creation
- teacher availability management
- structured lesson organization

### Student management
- active and inactive students
- student-specific context
- teacher/student/parent role separation
- centralized teaching activity instead of scattered notes

### Public teacher presence
- professional public profiles
- teacher discovery
- subjects, rates and availability
- reviews and social proof

### Communication
- internal messaging
- notifications
- platform-native interaction between participants

### Online teaching
- integrated live lessons
- homework and resource workflows
- teaching activity designed to stay inside the same product

### LUNA — Eduvo's AI assistant

**LUNA** is the AI layer inside Eduvo.

Its direction includes:

- contextual help inside the platform
- natural-language navigation
- voice and text commands
- quick access to lessons, students and calendar information
- assistance with repetitive teacher workflows
- a more conversational way to operate the product

The objective is not to bolt a chatbot onto Eduvo, but to make AI part of the product interface itself.

## Product direction

Eduvo is being built toward a simple idea:

> **One workspace for the operational side of tutoring.**

Instead of forcing teachers to combine WhatsApp, spreadsheets, calendars, video meeting tools and separate booking systems, Eduvo aims to make the entire flow feel connected.
