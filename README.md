# Eduvo

**Status:** Open Beta  
**Product:** https://eduvo.ro  
**Portfolio:** https://trifarafael.com/projects/eduvo-platforma-meditatii  
**Creator / Developer:** [Trifa Rafael](https://trifarafael.com)

Eduvo is an all-in-one SaaS platform built for tutoring teachers who want to manage their work from one place instead of splitting it across messaging apps, calendars, spreadsheets, video tools and separate admin systems.

## What Eduvo does

Eduvo brings together the core workflows of a tutoring business:

- lesson scheduling and calendar management
- two-way booking and lesson confirmation
- active / inactive student management
- public teacher profiles
- teacher discovery
- internal messaging and notifications
- reviews
- separate teacher, student and parent roles
- integrated online lessons
- homework, resources and teaching workflows
- **LUNA**, Eduvo's AI assistant for contextual help, navigation and voice/text commands

The goal is to give tutors a single workspace for organizing students, lessons, communication and day-to-day teaching operations.

## Current stage

Eduvo is currently in **Open Beta** and is under active development.
